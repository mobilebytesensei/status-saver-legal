Privacy Policy

Status Saver — Save & Convert

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Status Saver ("the app", "we") is a local-first utility that lets you view and save WhatsApp statuses to your device and convert or compress your own media. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • The app has no account and no sign-in.
  • Your statuses and media are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload, collect, or store your files, photos, videos, or audio on any server.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

Information the app accesses on your device

  • Media access — with your permission, the app reads the WhatsApp .Statuses folder and media you pick (via the system picker or a granted folder) so it can show, save, convert, or compress them. This access stays on your device.
  • Saved files — files you choose to save are written to your device's Downloads/gallery. They are yours; the app never transmits them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a limited set of device and usage identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policy. See Google's advertising policy and Google Privacy Policy. Purchasing the ad-removal option disables ads.

Analytics & crash reporting

The app may use Google Firebase for basic, aggregated app-health and usage metrics (e.g. crashes, feature usage). This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location.
  • We do not upload your statuses, photos, videos, or audio.
  • We do not require an account.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can revoke media/folder permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • You can delete saved files from your device at any time.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier in your device's Google settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
